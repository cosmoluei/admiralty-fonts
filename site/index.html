Admiralty

A type family in three voices, Sans, Serif and Mono, for interfaces, reading, code, data, brands and signs. Weight, width and corner radius go anywhere in between.

Change the voice, keep the layout

Admiralty Sans and Admiralty Serif have the same letter widths. Switch a paragraph from one to the other and every line still breaks on the same word, so an app can give two kinds of text two voices without anything moving.
