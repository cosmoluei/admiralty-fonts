Admiralty Mono is the monospaced member of the Admiralty family, for code, logs, IDs and tables. Every character is 600 units wide. Letters that are easy to confuse, such as I, l, 1 and |, or O, 0 and o, have clearly different shapes.

It follows the same humanist skeleton and vertical proportions as Admiralty Sans and Admiralty Serif, so the three families sit well together on one line. Optional programming ligatures are in the ss01 stylistic set and keep the fixed width.

Admiralty Mono comes in weights from Regular (400) to Bold (700) with matching italics, and covers Latin (including the Latin Plus set), Latin Extended-A, Pinyin and Vietnamese. To contribute, see github.com/cosmoluei/admiralty-fonts.
