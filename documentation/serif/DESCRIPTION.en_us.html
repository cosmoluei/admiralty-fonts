Admiralty Serif is a low-contrast text serif for screens. Its serifs are short and its curves are open and steady, so it reads calmly at small sizes and in long paragraphs. The italic is a true cursive design, not a slanted roman.

It is part of the Admiralty family with Admiralty Sans and Admiralty Mono. Serif and Sans share the same letter widths and kerning in every weight, so a text can switch between them without any change in line breaks. This was made for a chat app where one voice is set in Serif and the other in Sans.

Admiralty Serif comes in weights from Regular (400) to Bold (700) with matching italics, and covers Latin (including the Latin Plus set), Latin Extended-A, Pinyin and Vietnamese. To contribute, see github.com/cosmoluei/admiralty-fonts.
