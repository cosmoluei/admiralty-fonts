Admiralty Sans is a calm humanist sans serif for interfaces and long reading on screens. It has open apertures, a large x-height and quiet, even spacing, so text stays easy to read at small sizes and comfortable over long sessions.

It is part of the Admiralty family with Admiralty Serif and Admiralty Mono. Sans and Serif share the same letter widths and kerning in every weight, so a text can switch between them without any change in line breaks. This was made for a chat app where one voice is set in Sans and the other in Serif.

Admiralty Sans comes in weights from Regular (400) to Bold (700) with matching italics, and covers Latin (including the Latin Plus set), Latin Extended-A, Pinyin and Vietnamese. To contribute, see github.com/cosmoluei/admiralty-fonts.
